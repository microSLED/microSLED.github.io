μSLED

Microelectronics System-Level Design

We explore how complex electronic systems are modeled, architected, verified, and implemented — from high-level specifications down to RTL, SoCs, hardware/software interfaces, and FPGA or ASIC-ready prototypes.

Research focus

From models to silicon-aware systems.

μSLED connects system-level methods with implementation constraints, bringing together architecture exploration, automation, verification, and experimental hardware platforms.

λ

High-Level Synthesis

Design-space exploration and C/C++ to hardware flows for accelerators, control-dominated systems, and rapid prototyping.

▣

RTL and SoC design

Architectural refinement, RTL implementation, and integration of heterogeneous IP blocks into complete systems-on-chip.

⇄

HW/SW co-design

Partitioning, interfaces, memory organization, and system software for efficient embedded and reconfigurable systems.

≈

Measurement systems

Instrument-aware digital platforms, signal chains, and reproducible prototypes for advanced microelectronic experiments.

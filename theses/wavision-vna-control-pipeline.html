Master thesis proposal

Hardware/software co-design of a VNA control pipeline

Design and prototype a modular control and data-acquisition pipeline for the Wavision VNA platform, combining embedded software, FPGA interfaces, and measurement automation.

HW/SWFPGAMeasurementPython

Motivation

Modern measurement platforms increasingly combine digital control, embedded processing, and flexible hardware acceleration. A clean control pipeline can make experiments easier to reproduce, automate, and extend.

Objectives

  • Define a modular software/hardware architecture.
  • Prototype data movement and control interfaces.
  • Evaluate latency, throughput, and usability.
  • Document the design for future students.

Suggested background

Digital design, basic FPGA knowledge, Python or C/C++, and interest in measurement systems. Previous experience with embedded Linux or HDL is useful but not mandatory.

Expected deliverables

A working prototype, documented architecture, reproducible experiments, measurements, and a final thesis report.
